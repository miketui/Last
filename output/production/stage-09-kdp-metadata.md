<p>You became a hairstylist because you believed your hands could do something meaningful. But somewhere between the bookings, the bills, and the burnout, the work started to feel smaller than the vision you carry.</p>

<p><b>Curls & Contemplation</b> is the professional development guide that meets you where you actually are — and shows you what becomes possible when you stop treating your craft as just a job.</p>

<p>Written by <b>Michael David Warren Jr.</b>, a London-based celebrity hairstylist whose career spans editorial work for Harper's Bazaar and W Magazine, styling for Nike campaigns, and years spent in the rooms where creative risk pays off — this book delivers what no beauty school teaches:</p>

This is not a quick-fix guide. It is an interactive journey built for stylists who are ready to invest in themselves as seriously as they invest in their clients.</p>

<p><b>Part I: Foundations</b> — Rediscover your creative identity and sharpen your technical edge.<br/>
