# STAGE 10 — POD DISTRIBUTION & PRICING

---

## Pricing Strategy

| Format | Channel | Price | Rationale |
|--------|---------|-------|-----------|
| **Paperback** | Amazon KDP | $29.99 | Premium POD pricing for 423-page, content-rich interactive workbook. Consistent with comparable professional development titles. |
| **Paperback** | Direct / Author Site | $19.99 | Incentivizes direct purchases; author retains higher margin. |
| **Kindle eBook** | Amazon | $9.99 | Standard entry point for professional nonfiction. Maximizes 70% royalty tier (applies to $2.99–$9.99). |

## Print Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Trim size | 6.69" × 9.61" (KDP Royal) |
| Interior | Black & white |
| Paper | White (KDP standard 50# paper) |
| Cover | Matte laminate |
| Binding | Perfect bound |
| Page count | ~423 |
| Bleed | No |

## KDP Royalty Estimates

### Paperback at $29.99 (60% royalty)
- Printing cost estimate: ~$7.50 (423 pages × ~$0.012/page + $0.85 fixed)
- Royalty per copy: ($29.99 × 0.60) – $7.50 = ~$10.49

### Kindle at $9.99 (70% royalty, delivery cost ~$0.15)
- Royalty per copy: ($9.99 × 0.70) – $0.15 = ~$6.84
