## 2. Back Cover Copy (150 words)

You became a stylist because you felt something in your hands that most people never find — a gift for making people see themselves again.

This book is for the part of your career that beauty school never prepared you for.

Across sixteen chapters, Michael David Warren Jr. maps the full landscape of a creative hairstyling career: from finding your voice and building real networks, to mastering the money side, navigating burnout, leading with intention, and understanding how AI and texture diversity are reshaping the profession.

Each chapter includes quizzes, worksheets, reflection prompts, and sourced endnotes — because reading about growth only works if you put pen to paper.

Whether you are freelancing from a kitchen table or styling on global sets, this book meets you where you are. And it asks the question that matters most: What kind of career will you build with what you already carry?
