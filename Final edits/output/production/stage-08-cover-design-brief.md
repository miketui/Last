## 2. Visual Direction

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Deep Teal #008080 (authority, calm, professionalism)
- **Accent**: Gold #D4AF37 (luxury, warmth, aspiration)
- **Secondary**: Jade #00A86B (growth, freshness)
- **Text**: Near-black #1a1a1a on cream #f9f7f2 backgrounds

### Typography
- **Title**: Cinzel Decorative — elegant serif display, all caps
- **Subtitle**: Montserrat — clean, modern sans-serif, upper/lowercase
- **Author name**: Montserrat or clean sans-serif

### Mood / References
- Sophisticated but accessible — not cold luxury, not DIY craft
- Blend of editorial beauty (think Harper's Bazaar spreads) with warm, personal feel
- Gold foil or emboss effect on title lettering
- Teal as dominant background tone with gold accents
- Clean negative space — the book covers dense material; the cover should breathe

### Image Concept Options
1. **Abstract curl pattern**: Flowing golden lines on deep teal — suggestive of hair texture without being literal
2. **Brushstroke motif**: Matches the interior brushstroke chapter-number decoration — teal watercolor wash with gold title overlay
3. **Photography**: Close-up of textured curls (coils, waves) shot in black-and-white with teal/gold color accent overlay
4. **Minimalist geometric**: Gold circles/arcs on teal — evoking hair's natural spiral geometry

### Recommended Direction: Option 2 (Brushstroke)
Aligns with the interior design language. The book uses a teal brushstroke with overlaid roman numerals for every chapter opener. Extending that visual to the cover creates brand consistency.

## 3. Cover Specifications

### Front Cover
- **Dimensions**: 6.69" × 9.61" (trim) + 0.125" bleed on all sides = 6.94" × 9.86"
- **Safe zone**: Keep critical elements at least 0.25" from trim
- **Title placement**: Upper third, centered
- **Author name**: Lower quarter, centered
- **Subtitle**: Beneath title, smaller weight

### Back Cover
- **Dimensions**: Same as front
- **Content**: 150-word blurb, author photo (Michael.jpeg available), barcode zone (lower right, 2" × 1.2"), publisher logo zone
- **Tone**: The back copy should match the /ghost standard — grounded, direct, no AI-flavored language

### Spine
- **Width**: ~1.05" (confirm with KDP calculator for exact page count)
- **Content**: Title (vertical, bottom-to-top reading direction), Author name, Publisher logo/mark
- **Colors**: Teal background, gold text

### Full Wrap
- **Total width**: Front (6.94") + Spine (~1.05") + Back (6.94") = ~14.93"
- **Total height**: 9.86" (with bleed)
- **Format**: PDF, 300 DPI minimum, CMYK
- **No crop marks** (KDP applies its own)
