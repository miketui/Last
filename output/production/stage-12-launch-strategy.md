# STAGE 12 — LAUNCH STRATEGY
## Curls & Contemplation: A Stylist's Interactive Journey

---

## Pre-Launch (4 weeks before pub date)

### Week -4: Foundation
- [ ] Finalize KDP account and upload EPUB + PDF interior + cover
- [ ] Set up Amazon Author Central page (bio, photo, A+ content)
- [ ] Create landing page on author website with email signup
- [ ] Draft 10 social posts for pre-launch content calendar
- [ ] Begin testimonial outreach (see contacts below)

### Week -3: Awareness
- [ ] Post "coming soon" reveal on Instagram/TikTok with cover image
- [ ] Share excerpt (the house-call anecdote from Ch I) as a standalone story post
- [ ] Contact beauty industry publications for review copies (Modern Salon, American Salon, Behind the Chair)
- [ ] Reach out to hairstylist podcasts for guest appearances

### Week -2: Anticipation
- [ ] Open pre-orders on Amazon (if available through KDP)
- [ ] Share behind-the-scenes content: writing process, why this book exists
- [ ] Post worksheet/quiz previews as Instagram carousel or TikTok content
- [ ] Email list announcement with pre-order link

### Week -1: Final Push
- [ ] Countdown posts daily
- [ ] Share "I forgot she was still in there" origin story as video content
- [ ] DM and tag industry contacts with advance copies
- [ ] Prepare launch day email and social assets

---

## Launch Week

### Day 1 (Pub Day)
- [ ] Publish across all formats (Kindle + Paperback)
- [ ] Email blast to full list with direct Amazon link
- [ ] Social media blitz: 3+ posts across platforms
- [ ] Request friends/family/network leave honest Amazon reviews
- [ ] Go live on Instagram to discuss the book

### Days 2-3
- [ ] Thank-you posts to early supporters
- [ ] Share first reader reactions and screenshots
- [ ] Cross-post any media coverage or podcast drops

### Days 4-7
- [ ] Run Amazon Sponsored Product ad targeting beauty/hairstyling keywords
- [ ] Share chapter-specific content (one tip per day from different chapters)
- [ ] Engage with every comment and review personally

---

## Post-Launch Sustain (Weeks 2-8)

### Ongoing Content
- Weekly "Stylist Tip Tuesday" posts drawn from book chapters
- Monthly Instagram Live / TikTok session covering one chapter topic
- Share user-generated content from readers using worksheets

### Amazon Optimization
- [ ] Run KDP Kindle Countdown Deal (week 3 or 4)
- [ ] Enroll in KDP Select for Kindle Unlimited visibility
- [ ] A/B test Amazon description and keywords monthly
- [ ] Request editorial review placement via Amazon Vine (if eligible)

### Community Building
- [ ] Create a private Facebook or Discord group for readers ("Curls & Contemplation Community")
- [ ] Encourage readers to share completed worksheets with hashtag #CurlsAndContemplation
- [ ] Offer monthly virtual "study group" sessions walking through chapters
