Tools NOT available: `pdfinfo`, `pdftotext`, `pdffonts`, `qpdf`, `exiftool`, `identify`/ImageMagick, `pikepdf`, `pypdf`, `PyMuPDF`. ## Critical observations

### Multiple, materially different PDFs in `final/`
- 348 vs 581 vs 579 vs 587 pages. These are not minor variants; they were generated by different engines (Chromium vs WeasyPrint) and almost certainly from different print CSS. Only ONE can be the upload candidate.

### KDP-ROYAL-FINAL.pdf — possible non-embedded fonts (HIGH)
- 45 `/FontDescriptor` objects discovered; 42 carry `/FontFile`, `/FontFile2`, or `/FontFile3`. The remaining 3 may be Type 1 / Standard fonts (Helvetica, Times, Courier — the PDF "base 14") that are normally not embedded.
- KDP rejects PDFs containing any non-embedded font, including the base 14. **Run `pdffonts CurlsAndContemplation-KDP-ROYAL-FINAL.pdf` outside this sandbox to confirm names; reject if any reports `emb=no`.**

### KDP-ROYAL-FINAL.pdf — no `/TrimBox` or `/BleedBox` (MEDIUM)
- KDP technically does not require a TrimBox on no-bleed interior PDFs, but most POD vendors (IngramSpark, Lulu, Barnes & Noble Press) prefer one. Confirm vendor requirements.

### WeasyPrint PDFs — clean fonts and trim/bleed (✅ for those checks)
- All 12 `/FontDescriptor` objects are embedded.
- `/TrimBox` and `/BleedBox` are present. `/CropBox` is not, which is acceptable.

### All four PDFs are NOT tagged (no `/MarkInfo /Marked true`, no `/StructTreeRoot`)
- Untagged PDFs fail accessibility audits and won't qualify for screen-reader-friendly EPUB-replacement workflows. For a print interior this is acceptable; for an "accessible PDF" deliverable it is not.

### Encryption / corruption
- None of the four PDFs is encrypted. Header / xref / trailer parses cleanly on all four.

### XObject inventories (sanity)
- KDP-ROYAL: 119 image XObjects, 19 form XObjects, 1 ICC profile. (Higher because Chromium may have rasterized custom backgrounds.)
- WeasyPrint V2/V3 + interior: 19–20 image XObjects, 8 form XObjects, 0 ICC profile.

## What the audit could NOT determine

- Whether body text is correctly laid out (no blank pages, no orphans/widows, no chapter starts on left page when right-only is required).
- Whether headers/footers swallow the chapter title or page number.
- Whether the cover image is correctly embedded and not cropped.
- Whether colour images are RGB or CMYK (POD vendors usually require either RGB-with-ICC or CMYK).

These items are listed in `06_MANUAL_VISUAL_QA_CHECKLIST.md`.
