## Observations (not errors)

- Several endnote "accessed" dates and a few article dates fall in early 2026
  (Jan–Mar 2026). These are internally consistent with the manuscript's stated
  timeframe (mid-2026) and are not anachronisms; just confirm they match the
  intended publication date before release.
- Claims are attributed to named, reputable outlets (Allure, Fashionista,
  Essence, Modern Salon, British Vogue, Forbes, McKinsey, peer-reviewed
  journals). No fabricated-looking citations were found in the sample.

## Recommendation

No factual corrections required from the sampled high-visibility claims. For a
publisher-grade guarantee, a line-by-line check of every dated statistic and
quotation against its cited source is advisable before final release — that
full pass was outside this layout/QA scope.
