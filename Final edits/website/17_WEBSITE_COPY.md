# Website Copy — Curls & Contemplation

**What this file is.** The actual words for the highest-stakes surfaces — the home hero and the
`/book` sales page. The rest of the bundle specifies the machinery that *delivers* a sale; this
file specifies what *earns* it. Added in **v3** per the LLM Council review, which found the
bundle had no sales copy anywhere — only SEO meta tags.

**Voice.** Michael talking to a freelance hairstylist over coffee. Confident, direct, specific.
No "elevate your craft," no "unlock your potential," no "in today's fast-paced world," no
em-dash-stuffed AI cadence. Run every block through `humanizer` and `brand-voice:enforce-voice`
before it ships.

**Claims.** Every reference to Rihanna / IPPY / Guido Palau / Jimmy Paul below is **subject to
`claims-evidence.md`** — that file is the hard gate. If a claim is not substantiated there, it
does not ship here either.

**Pricing in this copy.** $15.99 pre-order (the 90-day campaign) → $17.99 regular. The Amazon
Kindle edition is $9.99 (bare ebook). The direct site wins on the **bonus bundle**, not on price
— never undercut Amazon, or Amazon can price-match the Kindle edition down.

## 2. The Book (`/book`) — sales page

This is the page that earns the sale. The order of the blocks is the argument — keep it.

### 2.1 Hero block

**Headline:** The craft made you good. This book makes it a career.

**Sub:** You can cut, color, and style. You can make a client glow. What no one sat you down
and taught you: what to charge, how to raise it without losing the chair, how to build a name
that books itself, and how to do all of it without burning out by year five. That is the whole

**CTA:** Pre-order — $15.99

### 2.2 The problem — who this is for

You went freelance (or you're about to) because you're good and you wanted the upside. Then the
real job showed up: pricing yourself, chasing invoices, finding the next client, saying no,
protecting your body and your time. The technique had a school. This part never did.

Curls & Contemplation is for the hairstylist who is **done guessing at the business** — who
wants the rate, the network, and the longevity to be deliberate instead of lucky.

### 2.3 What's inside — 16 chapters, 4 parts

Each chapter ends with work you actually do, not theory you nod at.

**Part I — Foundations of Creative Excellence**
1. *Unveiling Your Creative Odyssey* — name the career you're actually building.
2. *Refining Your Creative Toolkit* — what belongs in the kit, what's just weight.
3. *Reigniting Your Creative Fire* — plateaus, burnout, and staying in love with the work.
4. *The Art of Networking* — build the room you want to walk into, without the ick.

**Part II — Growing Your Craft and Career**
5. *Cultivating Excellence Through Mentorship* — find a mentor, be one, lose no time.
6. *Mastering the Business of Hairstyling* — rates, contracts, taxes: the most-bookmarked chapter.
7. *Embracing Wellness and Self-Care* — the body the chair takes back, and how to give it less.
8. *Advancing Skills Through Education* — the CE habit that keeps you bookable for 20 years.

**Part III — Leadership and Legacy**
9. *Stepping Into Leadership* — when the chair becomes a team.
10. *Crafting Enduring Legacies* — what gets remembered after the last booking.
11. *Advanced Digital Strategies* — owned audience, paid audience, what to actually post.
12. *Financial Wisdom* — cash, taxes, retirement, the second venture.

**Part IV — The Future of the Craft**
13. *Ethics and Sustainability* — the product-choice question that has gotten serious.
14. *The Impact of AI on Beauty* — what AI takes, what it can't, how to stay valuable.
15. *Resilience and Well-Being* — what makes a long career possible.
16. *Tresses and Textures* — the portfolio you owe every head of hair in your chair.

### 2.4 Why $15.99 — and why buy here, not Amazon

Straight answer: the Kindle edition on Amazon is **$9.99**. This page is **$15.99** during the
90-day pre-order. The difference isn't markup — it's everything Amazon's bare ebook doesn't
include. Buy direct and you get the **bonus bundle** below. Buy on Amazon and you get the book,
in one format, and that's it. Both are fine. This page is the better deal if you'll use the
extras.

After the book launches, the pre-order price ends and the regular price is **$17.99**.

- You want a franchise-in-a-box blueprint. This is a craft-and-business book, not a system to copy.
- You won't write your own emails, rate sheets, or contracts. The book hands you the words; you still have to send them.

2. **The bonus chapter is pre-order-only.** After launch it's gone. It does not come back, and it is never on Amazon.

That's it. If now isn't your moment, the Sample Chapter is free and the book will still be here.

**CTA:** Pre-order — $15.99 · instant EPUB + PDF + bonuses

### 2.8 Objection FAQ

**"Why pay $15.99 here when Amazon has it for $9.99?"**
The $9.99 Kindle file is the book alone, in one format. $15.99 here is the book in both formats
*plus* the Pricing Confidence Kit, the Chapter Workbook, free lifetime updates, and the
pre-order-only bonus chapter. If you'll use the extras, direct is the better value. If you only
want the bare ebook, Amazon is fine — no hard feelings.

**"Can't I just learn this on YouTube and Instagram?"**
Some of it, in pieces, scattered across a hundred creators with a hundred agendas. The book is
one freelance hairstylist's complete system, in order, with the exercises. You're paying for the
sequence and the hours you don't spend assembling it yourself.

**"I'm still in a salon — not freelance yet."**
Then this is the book to read *before* you leave. Parts I and II are the foundation; Chapter 6
is the business reality most stylists meet the hard way, after the jump.

**"Will it go out of date?"**
The craft and business fundamentals don't. And direct buyers get free lifetime updates — when a
new edition ships, it's yours, no repurchase.

**"What if it's not for me?"**
30-day refund, no questions. (See `/refund-policy`.) Your download access ends with the refund;
the rest is on the honor system, like every ebook.

**"What do I get, and when?"**
Pre-order now and you get the EPUB + PDF + the full bonus bundle the morning the book launches —
plus the free Sample Chapter (Chapter 1) immediately, so you can start today.

**"Can I read it on a Kindle?"**
Yes. You get an EPUB — open it in Kindle for Mac/PC, or use Amazon's free "Send to Kindle" to
push it to your device. The PDF reads anywhere.

## 3. Copy guardrails

- One primary CTA per screen. The primary CTA is always the pre-order; "Read Chapter 1 free" is always secondary.
- Never call it "buy now" while it's a pre-order — FTC. It's "Pre-order" until launch day (`14_SECURITY_LEGAL_QA.md § B.2`).
- Never inflate a claim: "IPPY submission" stays "submission" until a real result lands (`claims-evidence.md`).
- Never invent a testimonial or a number. Real names with written consent only, or nothing.
- The price in copy and the price in Stripe are the same number, server-computed (`04_BOOK_DATA_PATCH.md`). If they ever disagree, the page is wrong — fix the page, don't ship the mismatch.
